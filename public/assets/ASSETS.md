## Green arena tent

`props/green-tent-side.png` is the active arena tent, edited with built-in Image Gen from `green-tent.png` to face side-on with its triangular entrance at the right. It preserves the green fabric, folds, stitching, stakes and transparent background. Display size, spawn-relative placement and collision/doorway geometry are configured separately in the level's `tent` settings. The original image below is retained as the edit source.

## Arena map

`maps/arena-map.png` is a 1536 by 1024 full-bleed pixel-art landscape generated with the built-in Image Gen tool. The user's sketch defines its biome arrangement. Arena labels, ellipse outlines, selection and hover effects are rendered in code, not baked into the image. The grassland button repeats the existing `terrain/grass.png` tile. Edit or replace the landscape in an image editor and adjust the arena percentages in `src/content/game.json` to keep the buttons aligned.

### Arena map source prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production full-screen world map background for a top-down pixel-art browser game, landscape 3:2 ratio. No text, no UI, no circles, no labels, no border: code will overlay five elliptical arena selection buttons.
Composition follows a specific map sketch: dark green woodland fills upper left 45% width and upper 55% height, with dense rounded tree crowns seen strictly from bird's-eye view. Central small brown rustic town region around x39%, y51%, extending from x25% to55% and y22% to77%, irregular winding edges. Large grey city biome fills upper right, from x48% to100%, y0% to69%. Light green grassland fills lower left x0%-48%, y55%-100%. Sandy golden beach occupies lower right x47%-100%, y67%-95%, with a curving blue sea strip along bottom right edge y91%-100%. ALL biomes join naturally with ragged soft vegetation edges, sparse trees thinning to grass, patches of earth between grass and town, gravel blending into sand; no straight dividing lines, no geometric color blocks. Town and city are textured ground seen overhead: town warm brown worn earth with very small rustic terracotta and timber rooftops at the edges, city cool grey paving with very small grey rooftops at edges. Main biome interiors mostly ground texture so code buttons can show the same texture beneath them.
Art direction: crisp small square pixels, charming restrained retro farming game world map, small-scale fine texture. Grass must closely match existing game base grass #72a449 with sparse pale yellow and darker green tiny flecks. Woodland dark leafy green bird's eye canopy. City muted cool grey. Town weathered warm brown. Beach pale golden sand, subtle stippling, blue shallow water and thin foamy shoreline at bottom right. Consistent overhead lighting, natural-looking connected landscape. Full bleed. No perspective, no clouds, no mountains, no text, no roads dominating the composition, no decorative compass or legend.

The player and base grass artwork were generated with the built-in Image Gen tool and prepared with Sharp outside the game. Neither tool is used at runtime or added as a game dependency. The long-grass sheet is hand-authored pixel artwork in SVG format.

- `characters/boy-eight-directions.png`: the only player sheet, a transparent 72 × 160 PNG containing 24 × 32 frames in three columns and five rows. Rows face down, left, up, down-left and up-left; columns are idle and two walking poses. Right-facing directions mirror the corresponding left-facing row through `src/content/game.json`. All frames have a common foot baseline, with one transparent pixel below the feet. Edit or replace in Piskel or Aseprite, keeping the layout and alignment; update the JSON if changing the layout.

Shared by Grassland and Level 1 through `world.paths`; paths are clipped and repeated in world coordinates. Edit in a vector editor or replace with a seamless PNG and update the texture setting. - `terrain/cobblestone.svg`: retained original hand-authored repeating 32 × 32 pixel SVG with irregular grey-beige stones, dark mortar and small highlights. It is available as an alternative path texture; the active paths use dirt-gravel.svg. Edit the SVG or replace it with a seamless PNG and update the configured texture path. - `terrain/long-grass.svg`: transparent 30 × 12 sheet containing three 10 × 12 pixel tufts, each with three or four uneven strands. Uses darker grass green (#4e7632) with subtle accents (#5c863b). Edit in a vector editor or replace with a transparent PNG sprite sheet; `world.longGrass` in `src/content/game.json` controls the path, frame dimensions, variant count and seeded placement. Frames read left to right. Tufts are static backdrop decoration and always render beneath the player and shadow.

The player is a boy with dark brown curtain hair parted in the centre, a white T-shirt with a red dragon on the back, black jeans, and black Vans-style skate shoes with white soles. At this resolution, the dragon and shoe details are suggestive pixel accents. The superseded farmer sheets are available in Git history and are not needed by the game.
